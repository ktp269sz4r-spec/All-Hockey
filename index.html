    Competitions

    World and international (3)

    World Cup

    The 2026 World Cup was held jointly in Belgium and the Netherlands from 15 to 30 August, with 16 men's and 16 women's teams. Germany won the men's title and Argentina the women's.

    Pro League

    The top national teams play home and away across a season.

    Olympics

    The next Games are in Los Angeles in 2028, with hockey at Carson Fields and 12 teams in each of the men's and women's tournaments. The USA qualify as hosts, and Belgium's men and the Netherlands' women have qualified through the Pro League.

    European competitions (2)

    Euro Hockey League

    Europe's top club competition. Kampong won the men's title and SCHC the women's in April 2026. The men's 2026-27 season starts with Round 1 in Eindhoven on 9 to 11 October, and the women go straight to the Final 12 at Easter.

    European championships

    EuroChamps27 will be played in London from 28 July to 8 August 2027, with 12 teams in each of the men's and women's events. The best-placed team earns a place at the 2028 Olympics.

    Jersey leagues

    Europe's domestic leagues (9)

    England

    The Premier Division is the top tier of the England Hockey League, with 12 teams in both the men's and women's competitions.

    Hoofdklasse

    The Netherlands' top league, with 12 teams in each of the men's and women's competitions. Now sponsored as the Staatsloterij Hoofdklasse.

    Bundesliga

    Germany's top league, with 12 teams in both the men's and women's competitions. Hamburger Polo Club won last season's men's title.

    Outdoor games run to early November, then the indoor season takes over until spring.

    Belgian Hockey League

    Belgium's top league, with 12 teams in both the men's and women's competitions. The top four reach the championship play-offs. Waterloo Ducks won last season's men's title and Gantoise the women's.

    Sponsored as the Carlsberg 0.0 Hockey League.

    Spanish leagues

    The Liga IATI (men's) and the Liga Iberdrola (women's), each with 12 teams. Real Club de Campo are the reigning men's champions.

    French Élite

    France's top field hockey league, with separate men's and women's Élite divisions run by the Fédération Française de Hockey.

    Scottish Premiership

    Scotland's top league, with 12 teams in both the men's and women's competitions. Edinburgh University won the men's title for the first time in 2026, and Watsonians the women's. The outdoor season pauses for indoor hockey in December and restarts on 27 February.

    Wales

    Wales has no separate national top-flight league, and many of its leading clubs play in England's league system. Cardiff & Met are in the 2026-27 men's Premier Division.

    Irish leagues

    The EY Hockey League is Ireland's top club competition, with men's and women's divisions from clubs across all four provinces. Lisnagarvey won the men's title and Loreto the women's in 2026.

    Rest of the world (5)

    Hockey India League

    A franchise league with men's and women's competitions. Last season had eight men's teams and four women's. Kalinga Lancers won the men's title and SG Pipers the women's.

    Next edition is planned for the winter. Fixtures and tables will be added once it starts.

    Hockey One (Australia)

    Australia's national franchise league, with men's and women's teams in eight cities. The 2026 season runs from 8 October to 6 December. HC Melbourne won the men's title and Perth Thundersticks the women's in 2025.

    Argentina

    The Torneo Metropolitano in Buenos Aires is the country's biggest domestic competition, with 14 teams in Primera A for both men and women. San Fernando won both titles last season. The regular season is ending and the playoffs decide the champions.

    USFHL (USA)

    The United States Field Hockey League runs adult club competitions for men and women, ending with a National Club Championship each July. In 2026 Lone Star FHC won the men's title and Olympic Club the women's.

    NCAA Division I (USA)

    College field hockey is the biggest women's competition in the US, played in the autumn. Northwestern are the defending national champions.

    The US has no professional field hockey league.

    About the data

    Scores, tables and fixtures are snapshots from each league's own website or federation, last gathered 6 to 8 October 2026. They don't update on their own, and past fixtures drop off automatically. Sources include England Hockey, the Staatsloterij Hoofdklasse, the Hockey-Bundesliga, the Carlsberg 0.0 Hockey League, the Spanish federation (RFEH), Guernsey Hockey and Jersey Hockey Limited on Pitchero, Hockey Australia, Scottish Hockey, the French federation, FIH and EuroHockey.

    Some leagues are partial: the French Élite tables, the Spanish and Scottish women's tables, and league-wide fixtures for Guernsey, Jersey and Scotland are still to come. The US table is a coaches' poll, not a league table.
