    About

    I'm a hockey player from Guernsey, and All Hockey is where I follow the game, from the island's pitches all the way to the World Cup.

    Why I built it

    Most hockey sites cover one league or one country, and the island leagues I actually play in barely appear anywhere. I wanted one place where Guernsey and Jersey sit next to the Premier Division, the Hoofdklasse and the World Cup, for men and for women.

    What's on the site

    News, fixtures and results, league tables, a page for each major competition, links to highlights, a Guernsey page and a search bar. It covers England, the Netherlands, Germany, Belgium, Spain, France, Scotland, Australia, India and the USA, plus Guernsey, Jersey and the international competitions.

    About me

    I've been playing Division 1 for a few years. With my club I've won two Division 1 titles, and we're chasing a third this season. Hockey has taken me abroad, including a trip to Barbados to play the U18 national team, and I've been to the England U18 trials, which I got a huge amount out of. I'm a sponsored player with Balling Hockey.

    Feedback

    This site is still being trialled. If something is wrong, missing or confusing, tell me through the feedback form, because that's how it gets better.
